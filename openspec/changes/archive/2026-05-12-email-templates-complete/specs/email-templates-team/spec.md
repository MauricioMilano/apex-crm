## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Send invitation email to new team member

The system SHALL send an invitation email when an admin invites a new team member, including a temporary password and login instructions.

#### Scenario: Email sent on team member invite
- **WHEN** `inviteTeamMember()` succeeds and SMTP is enabled
- **THEN** the system SHALL send an email using the `team-invite` template to the invited member's email

#### Scenario: Invite email variables populated
- **WHEN** a team invitation email is sent
- **THEN** the following variables SHALL be populated: `{{invitedName}}`, `{{email}}`, `{{tempPassword}}`, `{{orgName}}`, `{{invitedBy}}`, `{{loginUrl}}`

#### Scenario: Temp password is included in email
- **WHEN** a team invitation email is sent
- **THEN** the temporary password generated by `inviteTeamMember()` SHALL be included in the `{{tempPassword}}` variable

#### Scenario: Invite email skipped when SMTP disabled
- **WHEN** SMTP is disabled
- **THEN** no invitation email SHALL be sent, and the action SHALL return a warning that the user was created but no email was sent

### Requirement: Send welcome email when new organization is registered

The system SHALL send a welcome email to the admin when they register a new organization.

#### Scenario: Email sent on organization registration
- **WHEN** `registerUser()` succeeds and SMTP is enabled
- **THEN** the system SHALL send an email using the `welcome-admin` template to the admin's email

#### Scenario: Welcome admin email variables populated
- **WHEN** a welcome admin email is sent
- **THEN** the following variables SHALL be populated: `{{adminName}}`, `{{email}}`, `{{orgName}}`, `{{loginUrl}}`, `{{setupUrl}}`

#### Scenario: Welcome admin email skipped when SMTP disabled
- **WHEN** SMTP is not yet configured (fresh registration)
- **THEN** no email SHALL be sent — this is expected since the org hasn't configured SMTP yet
